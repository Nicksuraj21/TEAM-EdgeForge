About LifeLens VR

Accessibility fails not due to lack of technology, but due to a profound lack of perspective. LifeLens VR is a dedicated virtual reality empathy engine that allows students, healthcare professionals, architects, engineers, and policymakers to experience the friction of physical barriers firsthand.

Ethical Positioning: We never claim a short virtual simulation replaces lived disability experience. Rather, it serves as a powerful catalyst for empathy-driven universal design and real-world architectural and healthcare retrofits.
